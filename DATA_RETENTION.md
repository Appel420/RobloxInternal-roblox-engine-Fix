# Data retention and deletion

This document defines the retention approach for child-related data.

## Principles

- Minimize collection.
- Retain only what is required for the service and legal obligations.
- Delete child-related data as soon as it is no longer needed.
- Avoid retaining voice data unless there is a documented and lawful need.

## Include

- Retention periods for each data category
- Deletion triggers
- Backup deletion timing
- Manual deletion request process
- Audit logging for deletion events

## Guidance

- Keep retention periods explicit.
- Tie every retained field to a business or legal purpose.
- Review retention rules whenever product behavior changes.
