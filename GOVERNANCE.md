# Governance

This repository documents review and ownership expectations for safety-related changes.

## Include

- Document ownership and review cadence
- Approval requirements for policy changes
- Escalation paths for safety incidents
- Auditability for moderation and deletion decisions

## Guidance

- Require review before changing child-safety behavior.
- Keep policy text aligned with product behavior.
- Revisit governance whenever the data model or enforcement flow changes.
