# Privacy notice

This is a template for a children's privacy notice.

## Purpose

Explain what data is collected, why it is collected, how it is used, and how long it is retained.

## Minimum sections

- Types of data collected
- Whether voice, chat, or telemetry is collected
- How child data is used
- Whether data is shared with vendors or processors
- How long data is retained
- How parents or guardians can request access, deletion, or correction
- Contact information for privacy questions

## Guidance

- Keep the notice plain and easy to understand.
- Do not imply collection that does not happen.
- Update the notice whenever the product or data flow changes.
