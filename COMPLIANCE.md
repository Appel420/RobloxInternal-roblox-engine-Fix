# Compliance overview

This repository includes example child-safety and data-minimization guidance.

## Important

- This document is not legal advice.
- Final compliance depends on the product, service model, jurisdictions, data flows, and legal review.
- Any live service should align documentation, consent flows, privacy settings, retention rules, and account controls.

## Core documents

- Privacy notice
- Verifiable parental consent process
- Data retention and deletion policy
- Children's privacy checklist

## Operational requirements

- Collect only data that is necessary for the feature.
- Default child accounts to the most restrictive safe settings.
- Keep voice collection disabled unless explicitly justified and permitted.
- Provide a clear deletion path for parent or guardian requests where applicable.
- Review moderation and retention logic before shipping any child-facing feature.
